Tawwa | توّا is a curated gift-box business in Kuwait. “Tawwa” is Kuwaiti Arabic for “just now / right this second.” The intended audience is busy young adults in Kuwait, ages 18–35, who often need last-minute gifts. The product promise is ready-made gift boxes delivered fast. Brand voice: a quick-witted, calm friend; short, warm, lightly funny sentences. The initial website is a mock-data demo with no real payments.

**Why:** the user supplied this as durable product, audience, and launch-scope context.

**How to apply:** use it when writing Tawwa copy, planning localization, or making product decisions; distinguish demo checkout from live commerce.
