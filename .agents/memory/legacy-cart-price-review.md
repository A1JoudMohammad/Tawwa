---
name: Legacy cart price review
description: Why carts without trustworthy price history need explicit review.
---

Treat missing or invalid saved price history as requiring review, not as evidence that the shopper already accepted today's price.

**Why:** Older saved carts have no price history. Automatically assigning the current catalog price during migration would silently imply consent that never happened. Requiring review is the conservative choice even when the price may not actually have changed.

**How to apply:** Future cart migrations must preserve personalization and leave price acceptance to the shopper. Do not invent an old price for comparisons or use saved prices as authoritative quotes.
